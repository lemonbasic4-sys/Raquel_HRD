## Team handoff: Annual Evaluation Flow

The remaining Annual workflow tests are assigned to the team. Use this checklist to track their progress:

1. Department members rate their own Core Values and their co-employees. The employee's final Core Values rating is the average of ratings received.
2. Each employee self-rates KRA based on job requirements, provides justification, and submits it to the configured immediate head.
3. The immediate head reviews the KRA and justification, approves them, or returns them for revision.
4. The system computes and records the final rating: `(KRA x 80%) + (Core Values x 20%)`.
5. Confirm the final rating is communicated to the employee and available for performance feedback and HR decisions.
